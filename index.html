Timeline: Adam to Jesus

The genealogy from Luke 3:23-38, Adam through Jesus. Adam through Jacob use ages Genesis 5 and 11 state directly (lifespan bars to scale, in years since Adam's creation) — Genesis doesn't give ages for anyone born after Jacob, so that stretch continues as a linked, undated chain rather than guessed dates.

Continuing lineage (undated)

Judah to Jesus — no ages are stated in scripture for this stretch, so it's shown in order only.

Matthew's line (David to Jesus via Solomon)

Matthew 1 traces a different line from David to Jesus than Luke 3 does — through Solomon and the kings of Judah, not Nathan. Both converge on Joseph; see ldolphin.org/2adams.html for more on the two genealogies.
